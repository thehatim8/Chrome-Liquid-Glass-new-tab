Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how New Tab - GLASS WIDGETS handles user data.

1. Data We Collect

The extension stores your settings and content locally on your device using Chrome storage, such as:

  • Widget positions, sizes, visibility, and theme settings
  • Background image settings and dock configuration
  • Notes/todo content you create
  • Sports widget settings (including API keys you enter)

2. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored locally in your browser profile via chrome.storage.local. We do not operate our own backend server for this extension.

3. Network Requests

The extension may send requests only to services you use in features:

  • https://api.cricapi.com/* for sports scores (when you press Refresh)
  • Site favicon endpoints for dock icons

These providers may receive request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) as part of normal web requests.

4. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your local extension data with third parties except through direct API requests required by the features you use.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains in local storage until you remove it by resetting settings, clearing fields, or uninstalling the extension.

6. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to keep the extension secure, but no software can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

This extension is not specifically directed to children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, include a support contact in your Chrome Web Store listing and update this section with your active contact email.
