# Brag Plan: Chrome Liquid Glass New Tab (96s Master Showcase)

## What is this app?
Chrome Liquid Glass is an open-source Google Chrome extension that transforms the default sterile new tab into an aesthetic, high-performance liquid glass command center featuring customizable frosted glass cards, magnetic grid snapping, intelligent wallpaper subject awareness, and a macOS-style floating dock.

## The angle
You open dozens of new tabs every single day — why stare at an empty white search bar? Chrome Liquid Glass transforms your browser into a personalized command center tailored to your aesthetic, with real-time sports scores, an OpenRouter AI chat assistant ("Ask me anything, Hatim"), smart notes with an inline math solver, and 5 curated themes from midnight supercar dark mode to Kyoto serenity.

## Hook (0.0s - 11.5s)
A sterile default Chrome new tab ("Google Search") is suddenly stamped "BORING & STERILE" with an audible drop impact. The voiceover asks: *"What if your new tab was your ultimate command center?"* before transitioning into the Liquid Glass workspace.

## The 5 Hero Looks (Dedicated Full 16:9 Showcases)
1. **Look 1: Midnight Executive — Bentley Supercar (22.0s - 33.5s)**
   - Aesthetic: Deep black frosted glass with crimson racing accents.
   - Highlights: Live FIFA World Cup match scores, interactive calendar with date picker, quick-launch entertainment apps, and sleek macOS dock.
2. **Look 2: Kyoto Serenity — Mount Fuji Pagoda (33.5s - 44.5s)**
   - Aesthetic: Pure translucent light frosted glass floating over Mount Fuji and autumn maple leaves.
   - Highlights: Clean distraction-free workflow with multi-engine search bar (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing).
3. **Look 3: McLaren Amber & Smart Subject Detection (44.5s - 54.5s)**
   - Aesthetic: Warm golden/amber glass tint harmonizing with supercar paintwork.
   - Highlight Feature: **Intelligent Wallpaper Subject Detection** — the extension automatically detects the vehicle in your wallpaper and shifts widgets to the perimeter so the artwork is never obstructed.
4. **Look 4: Straw Hat Anime — Monkey D. Luffy (54.5s - 66.0s)**
   - Aesthetic: Warm cream/parchment frosted cards framing the anime artwork.
   - Highlights: Integrated AI Chat ("Ask me anything, Hatim" via OpenRouter), Notes widget with instant inline math evaluation (`(10+10=) ➔ 20`), and custom-pinned dock shortcuts (Notion, Gmail, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram).
5. **Look 5: Cyber Runway — Yellow Hypercar (66.0s - 76.0s)**
   - Aesthetic: Smoked obsidian dark glass designed for power users and dark-mode lovers.
   - Highlights: Bold digital clock (`19:00`), vibrant yellow action buttons on AI Chat, real-time match tracking, and high-contrast precision.

## Modular Freedom & Drag-and-Snap Grid (76.0s - 85.0s)
Interactive demonstration of layout flexibility: long-press any widget to unlock modular placement, dragging cards across magnetic grid guidelines, and dock icon bouncing hover physics.
