## Variant 2: LinkedIn / Professional Product Launch
Most of us look at Google Chrome's new tab page 50+ times every workday. Yet by default, it's just a sterile, blank white search bar.

We set out to build an intentional, beautiful command center for our browser: **Chrome Liquid Glass**.

Here is what makes it unique:
1. **Dynamic Frosted Glass Engine**: Ultra-fluid translucent glass cards with real backdrop blur that match the color palette of any wallpaper.
2. **Intelligent Wallpaper Awareness**: Got a supercar, anime character, or landscape photo? The extension automatically detects the main subject and moves widgets away so your artwork is never covered.
3. **Modular Magnetic Grid**: Long-press any widget to unlock full drag-and-drop freedom and snap it anywhere on the grid.
4. **Built-in Power Widgets**: Integrated AI chat powered by OpenRouter, notes with instant inline calculator solving, interactive calendar, live sports updates, and an authentic macOS-style floating dock.

Zero tracking, private, open-source, and free forever.
